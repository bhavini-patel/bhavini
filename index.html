About Me

The Shopify Developer Behind the Stores

I'm Bhavini Patel — a Senior Shopify Plus Developer with over 7 years of hands-on experience helping eCommerce brands solve real business problems through thoughtful, performance-driven development.

My work spans custom theme architecture, Shopify Plus solutions, headless builds, ERP and API integrations, and conversion-rate optimization. I've shipped 50+ stores across fashion, food, jewellery, VR entertainment, and lifestyle — for clients in North America, Europe, the Middle East, and beyond.
